The land remembers everything. What it gave. What it took. What was buried beneath it before anyone thought to ask.

Three generations of the Valdez family built something real in California's Central Valley — a vineyard, a legacy, a name that meant something. Then the men in gray suits came with water rights paperwork and a story that didn't add up.

Joaquin Valdez knows how to read land. He's about to learn how to read people who want it.

And somewhere beneath the Black Willow tree, something has been waiting since 1898.
